  Each of its 99 distinct fact citations is a numbered fact of

  as §6 and §7. The evidence is
  [`runs/T-4wrw/trace-check.md`](../../runs/T-4wrw/trace-check.md).
- `NFR-007` cites `F-0004#1`, the idea owner's answer to a gap question of the
  problem statement (Decision Point 2, `F-0001#11`), not a sentence of the problem
  statement itself.
- The check proves the form and the resolution of each citation. It does not
  prove that a fact supports its requirement: the review rounds of `T-wjq4` and
  `T-84r5` judged that.
